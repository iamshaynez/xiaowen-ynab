FEATURES

Money tracking, done right

From entry to review, budget to reports — every detail built to last.

Envelope assigning

Assign income into envelopes first, then check what's left before you spend — no more month-end surprises.

One-line logging

“Log lunch, ¥35.” “Dining this month?” — just tell the AI; it records, categorizes and totals for you.

WeChat · Feishu ready

No app to open — log, budget and read reports right in your chat. One message, wherever you are.

Reports at a glance

Net worth, trends, spending mix, top payees — see where the money goes, and feel the ground under you.

Painless reconciliation

Bulk categorize, quick clear, statement reconcile; refunds return to the original category — never as income.

Daily auto-backup

Your ledger backs up on schedule, kept locally and in the cloud — safe from device swaps and slips of the hand.

THE METHOD

Envelope budgeting, done right

Assign before you spend. Turn the budget into visible envelopes — not month-end regret.

Give every dollar a job

Assign income as it arrives until every dollar has a home. Spend with clarity, not guilt.

Embrace true expenses

Rent, insurance, travel — save monthly for the big stuff, and bills lose their sting.

Roll with it

Plans change? Move money between envelopes. The budget follows your life — not the other way around.

Watch your age of money

The longer your money sits, the calmer your finances — age of money is your breathing-room metric.

GET STARTED

Up and running in two minutes

Free and open source. Your data never leaves your machine — a laptop is all it takes.

From source
# Clone & install
$ git clone https://github.com/iamshaynez/xiaowen-ynab
$ cd xiaowen-ynab && npm install

# Start it, then open your browser to begin
$ npm run dev
Docker
# One command — data stays on your machine
$ docker compose up -d

# You just record; it handles the rest
